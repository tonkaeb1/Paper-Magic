About Paper Magic

Hi! My name is Cairstin. Welcome to Paper Magic!

Paper Magic is intended to be a collection of origami project tutorials. There are different levels of difficulty for differing levels of cognitive ability and dexterity because I hope to have a little something for everyone.

I started Paper Magic because I wanted to give caregivers, retirement home staff, and retirees a website full of beautiful and fun origami projects that can help engage cognitive function.

I lost my grandfather to Parkinson's dementia, and I wish I could have done some of these projects with him. Paper Magic is my way of helping others share these projects with their loved ones as they go through their loved ones journey with dementia.

Whether you are a retirement home staff member leading residents in creative activities, a caregiver who wants to spend time doing a beautiful project with your loved one, or a retiree looking for a fun way to pass the time, I'm glad you're here!
