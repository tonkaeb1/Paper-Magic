Training

A little preparation can make the activity more enjoyable for everyone.

This page will teach caregivers, family members, volunteers, and activity leaders how to use Paper Magic before beginning a project.

Paper Magic principle: Choose activities for the person, not a diagnosis. Dexterity, attention, visual-spatial ability, prior experience, and the amount of assistance available can all affect which project feels enjoyable.
